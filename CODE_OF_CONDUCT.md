# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Airlock welcomes people of every background and experience level. Contributors, maintainers, and users should be able to participate without harassment or intimidation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Accept constructive feedback and correct mistakes openly.
- Respect privacy. Never post credentials, account details, private prompts, or private repository content.
- Keep issues, reviews, and discussions focused on the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention
- Publishing another person's private information
- Deliberate disruption, spam, or repeated bad-faith arguments
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern

## Scope

This code applies in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, release discussions, and other spaces where someone represents the Airlock project.

## Reporting and enforcement

For an urgent or private conduct concern, use the repository's **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**. Start the title with `Conduct report`. Do not include the report in a public issue.

Maintainers will review reports privately, avoid unnecessary disclosure, and respond in proportion to the behavior. Possible actions include a warning, removal of content, temporary participation limits, or a permanent ban.

False reports made to harass someone may receive the same enforcement.
